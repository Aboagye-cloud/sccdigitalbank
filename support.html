Frequently Asked Questions

How do I securely reset my online account credentials?
To securely update or reset your login credentials, click on the "Forgot Password" resource on the portal gate view. You will instantly receive a system verification link containing a timed, secure multi-factor numeric passphrase via your registered mobile line or email index.
What should I do if I suspect fraudulent transaction behaviors?
If you spot or receive alert flags indicating transactions you did not initiate, please lock your operational card profile immediately via your user options interface. Once locked, reach out to our active technical team right away through customercare@sccdigitalbank.com.
Are there service charges applied to instant digital platform transfers?
At SCC Digital Bank, all foundational operational actions, real-time account-to-account clearings, and normal monthly portfolio maintenance procedures carry zero internal processing transaction fees. External international settlement rates may vary.
How long does it take for standard support inquiries to get reviewed?
Our internal ticketing system routes and queues submissions immediately. For official items sent directly during open service team hours (Monday through Friday, 08:00 to 17:00 GMT), our team aims to provide comprehensive written updates within 2 to 4 business hours.
